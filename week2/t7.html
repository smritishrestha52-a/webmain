How to Make Pani Puri

Ingredients

  • Pani puri shells
  • Potatoes
  • Chickpeas
  • Spicy water
  • Chaat masala

Steps

  1. Boil the potatoes and mash them.
  2. Prepare the filling.
    • Mix potatoes and chickpeas.
    • Add a little chaat masala.
  3. Make the pani using spicy water.
  4. Make a small hole in the puri and add the filling and pani.
  5. Enjoy your fresh pani puri!
